About Hospital

The parent organization started its journey in the year 1979 as Chattogram Shishu Hospital. It was a humble beginning in an unfinished government building in Agrabad with permission of Ministry of Health. Later it expanded step by step and became a 150 bed hospital in 1988. In 1994 maternity services were added and the hospital was renamed as “Chattogram Maa-O-Shishu Hospital”. At present it is a 650-bedded hospital including Pediatrics, Neonatology, Surgery, Gynecology, Medicine and many departments. OPD services serve 1000–1500 patients daily with emergency services available 24/7. Radiology, X-ray, Ultrasound (2D & 3D with Color Doppler), Clinical Pathology, Hematology, Biochemistry and Blood Transfusion services are available round the clock.
Our mission is to attain international standard healthcare services in Maternal and Child Health care along with advanced services in all departments including surgery, medicine, ENT, dermatology, psychiatry and more. Our vision is to convert this area into a world-class Medical Village with international standard teaching, learning, research and service in both preventive and curative medicine.
The hospital has about 10435 life members who elect a 24-member committee every 3 years. The committee manages hospital functions through sub-groups. All policies are implemented by officers and staff.
Executive Committee includes: President (1), Vice President (3), General Secretary (1), Joint General Secretary (1), Treasurer (1), Joint Treasurer (1), Organising Secretary (1), Sports & Cultural Secretary (1), Members (13), Donor Members (2), Govt Representative (1), Ex Officio Directors (Admin & Academic).
The hospital is funded through donations, services income, NGOs, international organizations and zakat funds. Mega donors include PHP Group, Unilever Bangladesh, Rotary Club, Lions Club, Apex Club, KAFCO, Inner Wheel Club and others.
Funds are allocated into: General Fund, Trust Fund, Recurrent Expenditure Fund, Zakat Fund, Patient Welfare Fund, Employee Welfare Fund and Neonatology Development Fund.
A master layout plan is in progress on 4.2637 acres of land leased by Government of Bangladesh. The aim is to build a complete Medical Village with modern infrastructure.
